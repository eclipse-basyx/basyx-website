  <div class=""><h2>Eclipse BaSyx: Highly efficient product data management system thanks to open source AAS platform</h2></div>
  <p>The HARTING Technology Group sees the Asset Administration Shell (AAS) as a centralized approach to product data management across the entire product life cycle. The company uses Eclipse BaSyx to generate, provide and visualise AAS for its products and makes them available for downstream processes on the customer side.</p>
  <p>According to HARTING’s experience, BaSyx offers the following advantages:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><b>Cost efficiency</b> 
    BaSyx as open-source software results in significant savings on license fees.
    </li>     
    <li><b>Flexibility and adaptability</b>    
    HARTING can adapt and enhance BaSyx to their specific needs.</li>     
    <li><b>Security and transparency</b>    
    Third-party reviews enable companies to identify potential security vulnerabilities more quickly.
    </li>      
    <li><b>Independence from providers</b>
    The use of open- source software makes you independent of specific software providers. The risk of lock-in effects is reduced, and companies retain control over their IT infrastructure.
    </li>
  </ul>
  <p>In addition to these benefits, BaSyx paves the way to meet the regulatory requirements of the Digital Product Passport (DPP). Providing Asset Administration Shells as AASX file format or via API ensures the legally required interoperable data exchange format.</p>
  <p>At the same time, the visualisation enables the customer to view product specifications, e.g. sustainability aspects, in an easy-to-read and understandable form. This visualization also serves as a bridge to product-specific digital services.</p>
  <p>The use of BaSyx helps HARTING to secure and expand
its position as an innovation leader in the field of Industry 4.0 and to promote the efficiency, compliance and sustainability of applications through state-of-the-art industrial connectivity solutions.</p>
  <h3>About HARTING Technology Group</h3>
  <p>The HARTING Technology Group is a global leader in industrial connectivity solutions for data, signals, and power. The company develops and manufactures products including connectors, cable assemblies and network components for industries such as machinery, automation, transportation and energy.</p>
